01 Live on Google Play

Idey

A two-sided service marketplace. Clients post jobs and hire verified local providers. Providers subscribe, apply to jobs, and manage their bookings.

Role: Sole Flutter developer (freelance contract), working with a UI/UX designer and a full-stack developer.

Architecture
GetX for state, routing and dependency injection, with each feature split into controllers, bindings, repositories and models. Client and provider sides live in separate feature folders on top of a shared core. Networking runs on Dio.
What I built
Real-time chat with voice notes and live notifications over Socket.IO. Subscription payments through Paystack, Korapay and Interswitch. Didit biometric identity verification for providers, Cloudinary media uploads, and the app in four languages: English, Igbo, Spanish and German.
Getting it shipped
A GitHub Actions pipeline runs tests and lint on every push, with Codemagic for builds. Crashlytics catches crashes, and Remote Config with in-app updates moves users onto new versions. I ran closed testing with 12 testers, fixed what they found (including a broken OTP resend flow), and published to Google Play.

02 In Google Play review

Lábà FOODS

A food delivery marketplace for Nigerian staples, with one app for both roles. Customers order and track deliveries. Riders get turn-by-turn navigation to the customer.

Role: Flutter developer (freelance contract), taking the app to its production phase.

Architecture
Clean architecture with data, domain and presentation layers in every feature, and 37 use cases in the domain layer. Errors come back as typed Either results (dartz). Riverpod Notifiers handle state, get_it handles dependency injection, and GoRouter handles navigation. The backend is Node.js, Express and PostgreSQL.
Customer side
Google sign-in, Paystack checkout, and order tracking through Pending, Confirmed, On the Way and Delivered. Firestore powers in-app chat, and Firebase Cloud Messaging sends push notifications.
Rider side
Mapbox navigation with route drawing, distance and ETA, plus spoken turn-by-turn guidance through text-to-speech. The rider's location streams over Socket.IO so customers can track deliveries live. If GPS is off, the app asks the rider to turn it on.

Ridify

A geolocation app with a social layer: posts, profiles, a calendar and messaging. Built for a US-based team and now in internal testing.

Role: Flutter developer intern on a 6-person team, working under a senior mobile developer.

Architecture
Built on the Very Good CLI structure: state in Cubits from the Bloc library, with flutter_hooks, get_it and freezed models. The app builds in three flavors (development, staging and production), each with its own Firebase project. Messaging runs over WebSockets. Work goes through pull requests on Azure DevOps.
What I built
The message list and chat screens, plus profile, edit profile, settings, security, help and about screens. I integrated the APIs for the feed, calendar events, notifications, privacy settings, block and report, saved posts and My Posts, and added shimmer loading states.
Quality
Where the Figma board had gaps, I proposed and built the missing screens. I reviewed 150+ lines of Dart each week, which cut bugs by about 30%, and tested the app on Android and iOS ahead of release.
